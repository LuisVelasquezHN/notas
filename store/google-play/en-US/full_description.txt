LV Notes is a simple, private notes app. Everything is stored on your device and, if you want, syncs with your Google account. No ads.

WRITE YOUR WAY
• Rich notes: headings, bold, lists, checkboxes, quotes and code.
• Checklists: drag to reorder, add subtasks and hide completed items.
• Voice notes and photos inside any note.
• Templates for meetings, groceries, journaling, recipes or a trip.

EVERYTHING IN ITS PLACE
• Nested folders with color and icon.
• Tags, colors, pinned notes and favorites.
• Instant search and combinable filters (type, color, tag, date…).
• Smart folders: save a search and it keeps itself up to date.

NEVER FORGET
• Reminders on any note, including repeating ones (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly or yearly).
• Due dates on checklist items.
• Right from the notification: Done, Snooze 10 minutes or Open.

TRULY PRIVATE
• Without an account, your notes never leave your device; with one, they sync across your phone, computer and the web.
• Secure notes for passwords and sensitive details: encrypted with your master password, nobody else can read them.
• Lock the app or just some notes with Face ID, fingerprint or your device passcode.
• Full backup to a file you keep wherever you want, plus export to Markdown, plain text or PDF.

Plus: light and dark themes, Spanish and English, quick actions from the app icon, and undo everywhere.
